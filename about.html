A Century of Greatness: The Royal Legacy

Since its foundation in 1902, Real Madrid has been the golden standard of football. This is not just a club; it is a kingdom built on the sweat and glory of the greatest legends to ever touch a ball. From the pioneering days of Alfredo Di Stéfano and Ferenc Puskás, to the era of Zidane,xabi, Raul, and the unstoppable dominance of Cristiano Ronaldo. With a record-breaking 15 UEFA Champions League titles and 36 La Liga trophies, our history is written in gold. Under the lights of the Santiago Bernabéu, we have witnessed miracles that prove one thing: Real Madrid never gives up.
